## Known issues and follow-ups

- **Refresh-token reuse logs the user out.** If the app is killed after the server rotated the token but before the phone saved the new one, the next refresh is treated as reuse and every session is revoked. Safe, but a real user on a bad network could be signed out. Consider a short grace window for the immediately previous token.
- **Cover crop on iOS** keeps the photo's own shape: the iOS picker ignores the 16:9 aspect (Android honours it). The banner fills 16:9 by cropping on display. A custom cropper could come later.
- **R2 and Cloudflare Image Transformations** are coded and configured but not yet run against a real bucket; local disk storage was used for every test. First staging deploy must check an upload end to end.
- **Media worker** runs inside the API process when `RUN_WORKERS=true`; in production run it as its own container (`node dist/src/worker.js`, see `docs/deploy.md`).
- **Unused uploads** (a ticket created but never completed) are not cleaned up yet; add an R2 lifecycle rule on `uploads/` (1 day) at deploy.
- **Products go live without review** when the shop is active (decided 10 Nov 2026). Admins can hold or remove a product in prompt 20; a hold is never undone by the seller. One function (`moderationOnPublish`) changes this.
- **Left out of the product page on purpose:** Buy button (prompt 14), delivery fees, reviews and trust score (prompt 19), and any "money held" / Protected Pay copy. The listing flow has no "Speak in Tamil" or photo-drafted text yet, and no pickup slots step.
- **Profiles still counts live products and completed orders itself** for the shop header (it reads `products` and `orders` directly; now counts `completed`). It can't call OrdersService: orders imports profiles, and a cycle would need `forwardRef`. Kept as a documented exception; revisit with a read model.
- **Stock edits are compare-and-set:** every change says which stock it started from (`stockBefore`); if stock changed elsewhere the save is refused with `stock_changed` and the app reloads the real number. Checkout (prompt 14) holds stock without changing it; prompt 15 lowers stock on payment through the same locked path.
- **Product ids come from the phone** (random UUID v4) so a retried create is safe. Lists are ordered by `published_at`, not by id.
- **Feed reliability is only "verified" for now** (20% of the score). Completed orders, reviews and disputes join it in prompts 17-19.
- **Shops are placed to about 1 km** (`approx_location`, rounded to 2 decimals) everywhere in discovery: radius, distance and map pins. Two shops in the same cell show one pin on top of the other; distances under 1 km say "under 1 km".
- **Search transliteration** starts with about 70 common Tamil product words in `search_transliterations`. Add rows as real searches show gaps (no deploy needed).
- **Tamil trigrams depend on the database locale.** They work with `en_US.utf8` (local and Testcontainers). Check `SELECT show_trgm('முறுக்கு')` on RDS before launch.
- **Smooth 60 fps scrolling on a mid-range Android phone** isn't verified; the feed uses FlashList and image prefetch, but it was only tried on the iOS simulator.
- **Offline cache** keeps only the feed and categories (never your account, shop or orders) for 24 hours, and is cleared at sign-out together with recent searches.
- **Shop approval** is an admin action (prompt 20). Until then shops move from `kyc_pending` to `active` by hand.
- **Exact pickup address for buyers** is revealed only after an order is accepted (prompt 17). Today no buyer can see it.
- **Android maps** need `GOOGLE_MAPS_ANDROID_API_KEY`; without it the picker uses the phone's location only.
- **Android emulator** on this Mac (API 36.1) repeatedly lost its system services during testing; Android was verified up to launch and sign-in, iOS end to end.

- **Share links need a domain before they open the app.** Decided 11 Nov 2026: the domain isn't chosen yet, so it is a setting. Set `PUBLIC_WEB_URL`, `APPLE_TEAM_ID` and `ANDROID_CERT_SHA256` on the API and `EXPO_PUBLIC_WEB_URL` at app build time (see `docs/deploy.md`). Until then links point at the API and open the web preview page, whose "Open in app" button uses `xatu://`.
- **Shared links need an account** (decided 11 Nov 2026). A link opened while signed out waits (30 minutes at most) and opens right after sign-in. Only `/p/<uuid>` and `/s/<handle>` are accepted from outside; any other link just opens the app.
- **Reports wait for the admin console** (prompt 20). Each one writes a `report.created` outbox event; nobody acts on them in the app yet. Reports stay when an account is deleted (`reporter_id` is RESTRICT); prompt 21 must anonymise them instead.
- **Saved products and follows feed prompt 25** (back-in-stock and price-drop alerts, seller nudges) through `product.saved` and `shop.followed` outbox events. Saves of products that are later hidden are kept, but the Saved list shows only products buyers can see.
- **The shop page counts followers itself** (profiles owns follows, so this one is fine), but still counts products and orders directly (see above).

- **Open CA questions (prompt 14):** prices include GST and orders record totals as tax-inclusive with no GST breakdown; whether to split GST per item (and at what rates, since many home makers aren't GST-registered), and TCS on Xatu's commission, are for the CA. Commission is recorded per item at 0% until the rate is agreed (a new `commission_rules` row changes it; no deploy). Mithun should review the pricing and commission code before real money.
- **No buyer fee** (decided 11 Nov 2026): the buyer pays item prices plus the delivery fee. Commission is the seller-side cut, recorded for the ledger (prompt 16).
- **Local delivery has no area limit yet:** any buyer can choose it if the shop offers it. The maker can decline at acceptance (prompt 17); a delivery radius can come later.
- **Unpaid orders wait 15 minutes** holding stock, then a job (every minute) marks them `expired`. Checkout never depends on the job: holds past their time already don't count. There is no payment yet, so every order ends cancelled or expired until prompt 15.
- **At most 3 unpaid orders per buyer** at a time, so nobody can tie up a maker's stock with orders they never pay.
- **Late payments (prompt 15, decided 12 Nov 2026):** a payment captured after the order's hold ran out places the order if stock is still there (re-checked under the lock); otherwise it's marked `refund_pending`. Buyer cancel works only on unpaid orders; cancelling a paid order needs the refund flow (prompt 16).
- **Sellers can't see orders yet** (prompt 17). Old `idempotency_records` (24-hour expiry) aren't cleaned up yet; add a daily delete.
- **Idempotency keys:** a retry with the same key gets the first answer, including a refusal like `price_changed`; the app makes a new key after any final answer.

- **Payments are not in the ledger yet** (prompt 16). Each `payment.verified` outbox row carries the amount, currency and Razorpay ids so prompt 16 can record them. Refunds are only marked (`refund_pending`); nothing is sent back to buyers until prompt 16.
- **Webhook fixtures are doc-derived** (no Razorpay account yet). Re-record them from real test-mode deliveries once keys exist (`apps/api/test/fixtures/razorpay/README.md`).
- **A real test-mode payment hasn't run** (no keys). Locally, Pay shows "Online payment isn't available right now" until `RAZORPAY_*` test keys are in `apps/api/.env`.
- **Outbox relay:** every outbox row from prompts 9–14 is delivered (and marked published) on first run; topics without a handler are simply marked published. Handlers run at least once, so they must be safe to repeat.
- **Second payment on a placed order** (paid twice) and **paid after cancel** are marked `refund_pending`.
